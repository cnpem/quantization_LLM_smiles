SMILES (Simplified Molecular Input Line Entry System) is a specification in form of a line notation for describing the structure of chemical molecules using short ASCII strings. Here are some examples of SMILES notations:

1. Water: O
2. Methane: C
3. Ethane: CCC
4. Benzene: c1ccccc1
5. Aspirin: CC(=O)OCc1ccccc1C(=O)O
